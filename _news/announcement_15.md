:star: Our paper [Online Allocation with Differential Privacy](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.17554v1) was accepted as **Spotlight** (acceptance rate 1.3%) by **NeurIPS 2026**! It defines the differential privacy metrics for online resource allocation problems, formally establishes the performance limits of privacy-robustness trade-offs, and provides a theoretically-sound meta framework.
